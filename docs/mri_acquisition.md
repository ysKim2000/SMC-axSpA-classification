# MRI acquisition (final cohort, n = 291)

Source: DICOM headers of all 291 patients in the final cohort (`<dicom_root>/<patient>/{T1,T2}`;
for patient `P-A` the FS T1 series was taken from the correctly labelled series 802 of the same study,
see §5). Generated by `src/common/dicom_metadata_summary.py`.
Both T1 and T2 series were available for all 291 patients (291/291); a total of 2,322 slices were analyzed.

---

## 1. Scanners and field strength

All examinations were performed at **3.0 T** (291/291, 100%); no 1.5 T studies were included.
Three scanner platforms from two vendors were used:

| Scanner | Vendor | Field strength | Patients | % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MAGNETOM Skyra | Siemens Healthineers | 3.0 T | 143 | 49.1 |
| Ingenia / Ingenia CX | Philips Healthcare | 3.0 T | 121 | 41.6 |
| Achieva | Philips Healthcare | 3.0 T | 27 | 9.3 |

Software versions: Siemens `syngo MR E11` (n = 118) and `syngo MR D13C` (n = 25);
Philips release `2.6.3`–`5.6.1` (14 distinct versions, most frequently `5.6.1` n = 45 and `5.4.1` n = 24).
Receive coils were vendor-standard surface/body arrays (`MULTI COIL` n = 101, `POSTERIOR` n = 27,
`SENSE_SPINE_15` n = 13, `Dual coil` n = 6, `SENSE-XL-Torso` n = 1; not recorded in the remainder).
Parallel imaging (Philips SENSE) was flagged in the protocol name in 39 T1 series.

## 2. Which FS T1 sequence was used

The "FS T1" sequence was a **2D fat-suppressed T1-weighted turbo/fast spin-echo (TSE/FSE) acquisition in the
oblique coronal plane**, aligned to the long axis of the sacrum. Specifically:

- **Readout:** spin-echo family with echo-train acceleration in **291/291** series
  (`ScanningSequence` = SE in 271, Dixon-type RM in 20; `EchoTrainLength` > 1 in all 291,
  median 3, range 2–7). It was therefore a **TSE/FSE**, not a conventional single-echo SE and
  not a gradient-echo sequence. `MRAcquisitionType` = 2D in all 291.
- **Fat suppression mechanism** (from `ScanOptions` / `ProtocolName`):

  | Method | Patients | % |
  |---|---|---|
  | Spectral fat saturation (frequency-selective, SPIR/SPAIR-type; `ScanOptions` FS or SAT1/FS) | 271 | 93.1 |
  | Dixon-based water–fat separation (Philips mDIXON/ssmDIXON) | 20 | 6.9 |

  For all 20 Dixon cases the series analyzed was verified to be the **water image**
  (`ImageType` component 3 = `W` in 19; the remaining case, `COR T1 DIXON FS`, is an
  `ORIGINAL\PRIMARY\M\NORM\DIS2D` magnitude reconstruction). No fat-only or in-phase Dixon map
  entered the dataset.

- **Protocol labels** encountered (harmonized as one FS T1 oblique coronal sequence):
  `COR T1 FS` (n = 150), `T1 FS COR SENSE` (30), `T1 FS COR` (27), `T1 FS oblique coronal` (26),
  `ssmDIXON T1 COR FS` (19), `OBL COR T1 FS` (18), plus 10 further vendor-specific spellings
  (`OBL-COR T1 FS TSE(-SENSE)`, `WIP COR T1 FS`, `COR T1 (DIXON/TSE) FS`, `FS T1 oblique coronal`).

Recommended wording: *"fat-suppressed T1-weighted turbo spin-echo (FS T1-TSE) images in the oblique
coronal plane; fat suppression was achieved by spectral (frequency-selective) saturation in 271 patients
and by Dixon water–fat separation in 20."*

Vendor-stratified FS T1 values: Philips (n = 148) TR 520 ms (467–707), TE 15.0 ms (10.0–24.3), ETL 3 (3–7);
Siemens (n = 143) TR 614 ms (450–781), TE 9.4 ms (9.0–11.0), ETL 3 (2–5).

\* The `FlipAngle` field is vendor-dependent for TSE: all 148 Philips series report the 90° excitation
angle, whereas all 143 Siemens series report a **refocusing** flip angle of 120–150° (FS T1: median 148°;
T2 STIR: median 120°). Excitation was 90° throughout. Report this as *"excitation flip angle 90°;
refocusing flip angle 120–150° on the Siemens platform"* rather than as a 90–150° range.

Slice thickness (4.0 mm) and slice spacing (4.4 mm, i.e. 0.4 mm interslice gap) were **identical across
all patients, both vendors and both sequences** — the geometric core of the protocol was stable.

The T2 sequence was a **2D short-tau inversion-recovery (STIR) TSE** in the same oblique coronal plane
(`ScanningSequence` IR or SE/IR in 291/291; TI 200–250 ms), used for bone-marrow-edema assessment.

## 4. Protocol variation across vendors and over time

The cohort is a **retrospective, multi-scanner, single-field-strength (3 T) series**, so parameters were
not prospectively harmonized. Systematic vendor differences were as follows:

| | Philips (n = 148) | Siemens (n = 143) |
|---|---|---|
| FS T1: TR (ms) | 520 (467–707) | 614 (450–781) |
| FS T1: TE (ms) | 15.0 (10.0–24.3) | 9.4 (9.0–11.0) |
| FS T1: ETL | 3 (3–7) | 3 (2–5) |
| T2 STIR: TR (ms) | 5235 (3884–6692) | 4780 (3950–5930) |
| T2 STIR: TE (ms) | 70.0 (48.0–97.5) | 37.0 (36.0–47.0) |
| T2 STIR: TI (ms) | 250 (200–250) | 220 (220–220) |

The most consequential change over the study period was the **introduction of Dixon-based fat suppression
on the Philips platforms** (`ssmDIXON T1 COR FS`, 20 patients, 6.9%), replacing spectral fat saturation
in later examinations. Reconstructed matrix size also varied (512×512 in 130 patients, 640×640 in 115,
1024×1024 in 24, other in 22 for FS T1); all images were resampled to 512×512 for analysis, so this
variation does not enter the model input.

`StudyDate` was removed during de-identification (all values `19000101`), so calendar dates of protocol
changes cannot be recovered from the DICOM headers; the Dixon subgroup should be described as a
protocol change rather than dated. `InstitutionName` was likewise anonymized (`Unknown` in all 291).

## 5. Series selection for patient `P-A`

This study (Philips Achieva, software 5.6.1) contains three Dixon-derived coronal series with the same
`StudyInstanceUID`. The correct FS T1 series is **SeriesNumber 802**:

| Series | ProtocolName | ImageType | TR / TE / ETL | Appearance | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **802** | **`ssmDIXON T1 COR FS`** | **W (water)** | **591 / 15 / 4** | fat-suppressed T1: bright marrow, sharp cortical margins | **used** |
| 706 | `sT2 mDIXON COR FS` | W (water) | 2500 / 80 / 16 | T2-weighted water image; low T1 contrast | not used |
| 805 | `ssmDIXON T1 COR FAT` | F (fat) | 591 / 15 / 4 | fat-only map: subcutaneous fat hyperintense, bone suppressed | not used |

The parameter tables in §3 use series 802 for this patient, so the reported FS T1 ranges
(TR 450–781 ms, TE 9.0–24.3 ms, ETL 2–7) describe the acquisitions of all 291 patients with no exclusion
and no outlying values. Series 805 is the fat-only Dixon reconstruction and is not a fat-suppressed image;
series 706 belongs to the T2 mDIXON acquisition.

Note for reproducibility: in the working image directory the slices for this patient were originally
exported from series 706 rather than 802 (7 slices). The metadata reported here reflect series 802;
if the analysis is rerun, the image export for this patient should be regenerated from series 802.

## 6. Suggested Methods paragraph

> All MRI examinations were acquired at 3.0 T on three scanner platforms: MAGNETOM Skyra (Siemens
> Healthineers; n = 143, 49.1%), Ingenia or Ingenia CX (Philips Healthcare; n = 121, 41.6%), and Achieva
> (Philips Healthcare; n = 27, 9.3%). Because the cohort was assembled retrospectively, imaging
> parameters were not prospectively harmonized. Two sequences in the oblique coronal plane were used:
> a fat-suppressed T1-weighted turbo spin-echo sequence (FS T1-TSE; TR 573 ms [range 450–781], TE 14.8 ms
> [9.0–24.3], echo train length 3 [2–7], one to three signal averages, 90° excitation flip angle) and a short-tau inversion-recovery
> T2-weighted TSE sequence (T2 STIR; TR 4978 ms [3884–6692], TE 57 ms [36–98], TI 230 ms [200–250]).
> Fat suppression on the FS T1-TSE sequence was achieved by frequency-selective spectral saturation in
> 271 patients (93.1%) and by Dixon water–fat separation in 20 patients (6.9%; water images), the latter
> reflecting a protocol change on the Philips platforms during the study period. Slice thickness was
> 4.0 mm with 4.4 mm slice spacing (0.4 mm gap) in all patients, with a field of view of 250 mm (200–250)
> and in-plane resolution of 0.45 mm (0.24–0.69) for FS T1-TSE and 0.49 mm (0.28–0.74) for T2 STIR.
> A median of 8 slices per patient (range 1–13; 2,322 slices in total) covering the sacroiliac joints was
> analyzed. All images were resampled to 512 × 512 pixels before model input.
